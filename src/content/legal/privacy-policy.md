# MyNia App – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2, 2026

## 1. Introduction

MyNia Health (“MyNia”, “we”, “our”, “us”) provides a digital health platform designed to help users manage, monitor, and better understand their health information through integrated healthcare services, connected medical devices, AI-assisted analysis, and healthcare professional collaboration.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, protect, and process your personal information when you use:

- The MyNia mobile application
- Associated web platforms and dashboards
- Integrated healthcare services
- Connected medical devices and linked services

By creating an account or using MyNia services, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to this Privacy Policy.

## 2. Scope of Services

MyNia operates as a digital health ecosystem supporting clinical and wellness workflows, including:

- Non-diagnostic health assessments and questionnaires
- Health professionals approved AI-assisted health analysis
- Telemedicine and healthcare provider interactions
- Electronic health records and longitudinal monitoring
- Device integration and remote monitoring tools
- Caregiver and dependent account management
- Health document management and sharing

## 4. Information We Collect

### 4.1 Personal and Account Information

We may collect:

- Full name
- Date of birth
- Gender
- Email address
- Phone number

- Government-issued identification documents

### 4.2 Health and Medical Information

We may collect:

Device-generated data under user consent securely transmitted to our systems for storage, monitoring, AI analysis, and clinical review.

We may automatically collect:

- IP address

### 4.5 Communication Information

We may collect:

- Chat messages
- Teleconsultation records

- Government-issued identification

## 6. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- Create and manage your account
- Provide healthcare and wellness services
- Store and organize your health records
- Enable telemedicine and provider interactions
- Synchronize connected health devices
- Generate monitoring dashboards and reports
- Improve system functionality and security
- Conduct analytics and service improvement
- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations

## 7. AI-Assisted Analysis

MyNia may use AI‑assisted systems to analyze de‑identified clinical data, including:

- ECG signals
- Medical images
- Stethoscope recordings
- Questionnaire responses
- Vital signs and trends

AI-generated outputs may include:

- Preliminary pattern detection
- Risk indicators and trend analysis
- Clinical decision‑support insights for authorised clinicians

### Important Safeguards

- AI outputs are not autonomous medical diagnoses
- AI outputs are intended solely for use by licensed healthcare professionals as part of their clinical decision‑making
- AI results may not be displayed directly to end‑users within the MyNia app
- AI systems are designed to support and not to replace professional clinical judgment
- All AI analysis uses de‑identified data. The de‑identified database will not be re‑identified.

## 8. Research, Analytics, and Model Improvement

MyNia may use de-identified and anonymized data for:

- AI model improvement
- Clinical and public health research
- Product development and quality improvement
- System analytics and performance monitoring

### Data Protection Commitments

- The de‑identified database will not be re‑identified
- While de‑identification significantly reduces privacy risk, a theoretical possibility of re‑identification exists when datasets are combined. MyNia implements strict technical, organisational, and governance safeguards to minimise this risk
- Neither MyNia nor its partners will attempt to re‑identify any de‑identified data, and re‑identification is prohibited under these Terms
- No directly identifiable personal information is used for analytics, research, or model training without a valid legal basis or explicit consent

Users may opt out of eligible research‑related processing through available settings or by contacting us.

### 9.4 Legal and Safety Obligations

## 10. Viewing, Downloading, and Sharing Health Information

### 10.1 Viewing Health Information

Health information accessed through MyNia is generally viewable only during active sessions unless intentionally downloaded by the user.

An internet connection may be required for real-time synchronization and access to the latest records

### 10.2 Downloading Health Information

Users may download selected health documents and reports to their device.

Downloaded documents:

- May remain stored on the user’s device
- May not automatically synchronize with updated records
- Are outside MyNia’s direct control once downloaded

Users are responsible for protecting downloaded files stored on their devices.

### 10.3 Sharing Health Information

MyNia may allow users to share health information, reports, or documents with other individuals or organizations.

Users should share information only with trusted recipients.

Important:

- Shared information may not be retrievable or revocable after sharing
- MyNia may not track all recipient actions after information is shared externally

#### 10.3.1 Family Members, Dependents, and Caregiver Access

MyNia may allow users to create or manage family groups within the platform.

##### Head of Family Group

The designated “Head” account may be granted the ability to:

- View and manage the health records of linked dependents or family members
- Manage permissions and access settings for linked dependent profiles and other member profiles
- Coordinate healthcare-related activities within the family group

The Head account holder is responsible for ensuring they have the legal authority or consent necessary to manage another individual’s information.

##### Independent Family Member Accounts

Certain family members may maintain their own independent MyNia account, including:

- Individual login credentials
- Separate personal health records
- Independent control over their own information and permissions

These users retain direct control over their own account and data.

##### Dependent Profiles

Dependent profiles may be created for individuals who are unable to independently manage their own account, including:

- Infants and young children
- Elderly family members under care
- Individuals requiring caregiver assistance

Dependent profiles may:

- Be linked to a Head account
- Be managed by an authorized caregiver or representative
- Share permissions and administrative controls through the Head account

Access and management rights may vary depending on the dependent’s age, legal status, and applicable regulations.

Your information may be processed or stored on secure servers located outside your country of residence.

## 15. Data Retention

We retain data:

- While your account remains active
- As necessary for healthcare, legal, regulatory, or safety obligations
- For legitimate business and audit purposes

When accounts are deleted:

- Personal identifiers may be deleted or anonymized
- Certain records may be retained where legally required
- De-identified information may remain for research or analytics purposes

## 16. Account Deletion and App Removal

### 16.1 Deleting Your Account

You may request account deletion:

- Through in-app settings (if available)
- By contacting support at privacy@gmedcc.com

Upon deletion request:

- Personal information may be deleted within a reasonable timeframe
- Certain medical or legal records may be retained where required by law

### 16.2 Removing the MyNia App

Deleting the MyNia application from your device does not automatically delete your account or backend health records.

After app removal:

- Downloaded files may remain on your device
- Previously shared information cannot be recalled
- Your health records may still exist within linked healthcare systems or MyNia infrastructure

## 17. Children’s Privacy

For users under 18 years old:

- Accounts must be managed by a parent, guardian, or authorized representative
- Additional protections may apply to children’s data
- Consent may be required before processing certain information

## 18. Pseudonyms and Anonymity

Certain healthcare services may require real identity verification for safety, regulatory, or clinical reasons.

As a result:

- MyNia may not support anonymous usage for core healthcare services
- Pseudonyms may be permitted only where legally and operationally appropriate
